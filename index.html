◈ About WalletScope

WalletScope is an advanced crypto wallet discovery and recovery tool. It scans blockchain networks for wallets with active balances, then uses a high-performance bruteforce engine to attempt recovery of the associated seed phrase. With Pro Access, you get unlimited scans, priority processing, and lifetime updates.

  • Find Wallets — Scan for wallets with non-zero balances
  • Recovery Engine — Attempt to crack recovered wallets
  • Pro Access — Unlock unlimited scans & priority support
